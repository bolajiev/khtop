This is khtop — the terminal dashboard for KeeperHub. One screen, always on: your workflows, executions, and wallet, live.

KeeperHub keeps your onchain agents alive — retries, gas handling, audit trails. But the only way to see any of it is the web app, or asking a chat plugin a question. khtop puts it all on one screen.

Every run, every step: the trigger, the simulation, the submitted transaction, the gas used. And when a run fails, the error is right there. No digging. No guessing.

And it executes onchain. Press t, enter an amount, and khtop simulates first — catching reverts before anything is broadcast. Confirm, and KeeperHub handles the rest: idempotent execution, gas handling, the full audit trail.
